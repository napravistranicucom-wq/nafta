Questions

Before you join

Is it really half the price?

Yes. Every Crewly plan costs half of the matching Housecall Pro plan, with the same number of users included. Extra users cost $17 a month instead of $35.

How does the free migration work?

You export your customers, jobs and price book from Housecall Pro. We import them into Crewly, then walk through the result with you before you cancel anything.

Does joining cost anything?

No. The waitlist is free and needs no card. You decide whether to sign up when your spot opens.

When can my crew start?

We are onboarding founding members in small groups so each shop gets a proper migration. We'll email you when your group opens.
